## Confidence note

**Not enough to build a reliable system prompt from yet.** Two short samples is far below what's
needed to confidently clone a voice — this draft exists so there's something concrete to react
to and correct, per the master prompt's own quality-gate step, not because it's ready to ship.

**Important open question for the business owner**: sample 1 (prices) reads like an AI
assistant confirming to *you* that it updated a "Knowledge"/"Catalog" system ("راني حدثت جميع
الأثمنة فـ Knowledge وفـ Catalog كيفما طلبتي" — "OK, I updated all the prices in Knowledge and
Catalog as you asked" — addressed to *you*, not to a customer). If that's true, it should **not**
be used as a voice sample at all — it would teach the reply system to imitate another AI tool's
phrasing instead of your own. Sample 2 (shop location) reads more plausibly like an actual
customer-facing reply, but I can't confirm who wrote it either. **Please confirm**: did you
personally write these, or were they generated by some other assistant/chatbot you were already
using? If the latter, ignore sample 1 entirely and send real examples of your own wording instead
(WhatsApp replies you've typed yourself are ideal).

## Language mix

Both samples are Darija written in Arabic script, with occasional English/French loanwords kept
in Latin script inline: "Knowledge", "Catalog", "Blackout". No French sentences observed. No
Latin-script Darija (Arabizi) observed in these samples.

## Register & tone

- Opens sample 2 by mirroring the customer's Islamic greeting: "وعليكم السلام ورحمة الله!"
- Business speaks of itself in first-person-plural framing: "المحل ديالنا" ("our shop").
- Both samples end with an open offer to help further:
  - "واش كاين شي حاجة أخرى نقدر نعاونك فيها؟" (sample 1)
  - "كيفاش نقدر نعاونك اليوم؟" (sample 2)
  This closing-question pattern appears in both samples, so it's the one thing with enough
  repetition (n=2) to flag as a possible real habit — still worth confirming with more samples
  before relying on it.

## Structure & recurring patterns

- Uses bullet points (•) with bolded numbers/prices via `*asterisks*` for a price list
  (sample 1).
- Prices formatted as `470.00 درهم` — two decimal places, درهم spelled out (not "MAD" or "DH").
- Gives concrete physical directions with a landmark reference in sample 2 ("حدا واحد القهوة في
  الطريق اللي كتخرج على الدراركة" — "next to a café on the road that goes out to Drarga")
  rather than just an address — suggests a preference for landmark-based directions over formal
  addresses, but this is a single data point.
- Shares a Google Maps link directly in-line rather than describing the route only in words.

## Explicitly NOT observed (do not assume)

- No emoji anywhere in either sample.
- No French sentences or phrases.
- No informal slang, abbreviations, or Arabizi (Latin-script Darija).
- No sign-off / closing salutation beyond the "how can I help further" question — no "شكرا",
  no name signature.
- No evidence either way on how he handles a genuinely angry/complaining customer message —
  both samples are neutral, informational exchanges.
