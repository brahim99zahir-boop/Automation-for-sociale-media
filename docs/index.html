Fiha Khir (فيها خير)

Made-to-measure mosquito screens — Agadir, Morocco.
Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, and Data Deletion. Last updated 2 August 2026.

Fiha Khir is a workshop in Agadir that manufactures made-to-measure mosquito screens (moustiquaires). We use an automated assistant to help answer questions left on our own Instagram posts and sent to our own Instagram account, so that customers get a reply outside working hours.

Privacy Policy

What we collect

Only what a person sends to us of their own accord:

  • The text of a comment left on one of our Instagram posts, or a direct message sent to our account
  • The public username attached to that comment or message
  • The time it was sent

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, location, contact lists, or anything from a person's own account or posts. We do not buy or receive data about anyone from any third party.

Why we use it

To answer the question. A customer asking the price of a screen receives our per-square-metre rate and is asked for the measurements of their window. Nothing else is done with it.

Who else sees it

  • Anthropic — the message text is sent to the Claude API to compose a reply in our own words. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Google — the automation runs on Google Apps Script and records messages in a private Google Sheet that only the business owner can open. See Google's privacy policy.

We never sell, rent, share, or publish anyone's data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

How long we keep it

Messages stay in the private log for as long as the enquiry is useful to us — typically one selling season. Older rows are deleted. Nothing is kept after a deletion request.

Security

The log is a private Google Sheet accessible only to the business owner's Google account. API credentials are held in Google's Script Properties and never appear in the source code.

Children

Our products and this service are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Terms of Service

This service exists so that people who contact Fiha Khir on Instagram receive an answer promptly. By sending us a comment or a message you accept the following.

  • Replies may be composed automatically. They are drafted from a fixed set of facts about our products, and are reviewed by us before the system is allowed to answer on its own.
  • A price quoted in a reply is a per-square-metre rate, or a calculation from the measurements you supplied. It is an estimate, not a contract. The final price is confirmed on WhatsApp before any work begins.
  • Orders, payment, delivery, and installation are agreed directly with us on WhatsApp at 0666567672, not through the automated replies.
  • Every screen is made to order from the measurements you provide. Please check them.
  • We may stop, change, or withdraw the automated replies at any time. It is a convenience, not an obligation, and it does not replace speaking to us.

Data Deletion

To have everything we hold about you deleted, contact us by either route:

  • WhatsApp: 0666567672
  • Instagram: a direct message to @fiha_khir13 asking for deletion

Send the Instagram username you used to contact us. We remove every record of your messages from our log within 30 days and confirm when it is done. No account or password is needed, and there is no charge.

You may also remove our app's access to your own Instagram account at any time from Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites.
